About Quiz

A server-rendered quiz platform for Indonesian classrooms: the teacher builds and runs a quiz, the class joins with a code, and everything updates live.

For guru

  • Question bank with multiple-choice, multi-answer and essay questions.
  • Compose quizzes with global or per-question timers, shuffling and attempt limits.
  • Live monitor: start and stop, manage participants, watch the ranking.
  • Anti-cheat events (tab switching, minimized window) recorded per attempt.
  • Results with Excel export, plus manual grading for essays.
  • Classes and majors lists, and student password-reset approvals.

For murid

  • Join with a 6-character code, an invite link or the class QR.
  • Answer from any phone — question order and timers are handled for you.
  • See the live ranking while the quiz runs, when your teacher enables it.
  • Review your answers and follow every attempt in History.
  • Profile with your class and major, plus self-service password change.
